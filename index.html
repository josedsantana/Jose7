About Me

Hello There!

I'm a Seventh-day Adventist Christian and a theology student at Weimar University in California with a desire to serve God and others. Above all, I want my life to point people to Jesus Christ.

My interests include theology, ministry, education, leadership, health and wellness, music, technology, and personal development. I enjoy studying and sharing the Bible, spending time in nature, exercising, learning new things, building meaningful relationships, and finding practical ways to serve others.
